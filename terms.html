1. The service

Synced Events Calendar ("the app") shows events from calendars you connect (a public Google Calendar, an iCal/ICS address, or your site's own events) in a calendar widget on your Wix site, with a dashboard for managing connected calendars. The app depends on the availability of those calendar feeds and on Wix platform services; features may change as those services change.

2. Plans and payment

The app offers a free plan and a paid Pro plan. Paid plans are purchased through Wix and billed by Wix according to the plan shown at checkout. You can cancel a paid plan from your Wix account; access to Pro features ends at the end of the paid period. If a site drops from Pro to the free plan, only the first connected calendar keeps showing until you choose which one to keep.
