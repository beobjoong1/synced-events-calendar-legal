Getting started (three steps)

  1. Connect a calendar. In your Wix dashboard open Apps → Google Calendar Events Sync, choose where your calendar lives and paste its address (directions below). Not ready yet? Click Use a sample calendar to see the widget working right away. No external calendar? In the editor's settings panel (or on the Your events page) add your first event right there — a title, a date and, if you like, a time. Visitors see it on the published site.
  2. Add the widget to a page. In the editor open Add → Apps → Google Calendar Events Sync and drop the widget onto a page. The widget's settings panel controls the view, colors, week start and time zone.
  3. Publish the site. From then on the calendar refreshes on its own: hourly on the Free plan, every 5 minutes on Pro.

Google Calendar

Public calendar (Calendar ID)

  1. Open Google Calendar on the web and click the gear icon → Settings.
  2. Under Settings for my calendars pick the calendar.
  3. In Access permissions for events tick Make available to public.
  4. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the Calendar ID (it looks like abc123@group.calendar.google.com or your Gmail address).
  5. In the app dashboard choose Google Calendar — public calendar and paste the ID.

Private calendar (secret address)

  1. Google Calendar → gear icon → Settings → pick the calendar.
  2. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format.
  3. In the app dashboard choose Google Calendar — private (secret address) and paste it.
The secret address is stored encrypted, shown masked in the dashboard and never sent to visitors. If you ever reset it in Google, paste the new address.

Outlook / Microsoft 365

  1. Open Outlook on the web → gear icon → Calendar → Shared calendars.
  2. Under Publish a calendar choose the calendar and Can view all details, then click Publish.
  3. Copy the ICS link (not the HTML link) and paste it in the app dashboard under Outlook / Microsoft 365.

Outlook feeds use Windows time-zone names; the app converts them, including daylight-saving changes.

Apple iCloud Calendar

  1. On iCloud.com → Calendar click the share icon next to the calendar.
  2. Tick Public Calendar and copy the link (it starts with webcal://).
  3. Paste it in the app dashboard under Apple iCloud Calendar. On a Mac: Calendar app → right-click the calendar → Share Calendar → Public Calendar.

Teamup and any other iCal feed

Teamup: open the menu → Preferences → iCalendar feeds and copy the feed of the sub-calendar you want to show (ends with .ics).

Anything else: most calendar tools (Eventbrite, Luma, Nextcloud, university calendars, WordPress event plugins…) offer an "iCal", "ICS" or "webcal" subscription link in their sharing or export settings. Paste that link; it must open without a login.

Wix Events

If the Wix Events app is installed on your site, choose Wix Events (this site) and click Connect. Published events appear with their registration status; the details show an RSVP / Register button that leads to the event page.

Your own events

No outside calendar needed: in the app dashboard open Your events (or click Or create events here) and choose Add an event. An event has a title, a date and time or All day, and optionally a location, a description and a link. It can repeat daily, weekly or monthly, until a date you pick. Times are kept in the event's time zone, and repeating events keep the same clock time across daylight saving. Your events appear in the widget next to any connected calendar, labelled Your events. On the Free plan they count as your one calendar.

Give an event categories — comma-separated, for example Kids, Music. On Pro the calendar shows category buttons so visitors can filter; in the widget settings you can leave a category out of one widget. Categories also come from Wix Events and iCal feeds.

Pictures (Pro): choose an image from your site's media; it shows in the event details.

Visitor suggestions (Pro)

Turn on Visitor suggestions on the Your events page and visitors see a Suggest an event button in the calendar. A suggestion has a title, a date and time, and optionally a location, a description and a link; the form does not ask for a name or email. Each one waits on Your events until you approve it (it becomes one of your events) or decline it (it is deleted) — nothing appears on your site before you approve it. The dashboard home tells you when suggestions are waiting. Up to 50 can wait at once. Turning suggestions off removes the button and keeps the ones already waiting.

Widget settings

  • Views: Month and List on every plan; Week and Day on Pro. On narrow screens the month grid switches to a readable list.
  • Style preset: Classic, Minimal, Bold, Dark or Pastel in one click. Your own accent color, text and background color, corner radius and font override the preset, on every plan.
  • Time zone: the visitor's zone, one fixed zone, or a zone you type (any IANA name such as Europe/Berlin).
  • Calendar chips, location, descriptions: show or hide them; a location that is an address comes with a map link; descriptions in list views can be one line, the full text or hidden.
  • Pro extras for visitors: a search box, pictures in the event details (for Wix Events and events you create here; Google Calendar and iCal feeds don't carry pictures), add-to-calendar buttons (Google, .ics), a Subscribe button that offers the whole calendar as a feed, and a Suggest an event button when you turn on visitor suggestions.

Events with a location are also published as structured data (JSON-LD) so search engines can list them. This is on for every plan.

Free and Pro

Free: one calendar (a connected calendar or the events you create), the next 100 upcoming events, month and list views, hourly refresh, no watermark. Sites that installed the app before this limit started on 12 October 2026 keep all their events. Connect a second calendar on Free and it is saved switched off — marked Waiting for Pro — and turns on by itself when you start Pro (up to nine waiting).

Schedule Assistant

Schedule Assistant includes everything in Pro and adds three things for people who run a programme — a church, a club, a school, a community group:

  • What changed. After every sync the dashboard lists the events that were added, cancelled or moved since you last looked, so you can tell your people without reading the whole calendar again. When something changed you also get a note in your Wix dashboard.
  • This week's draft. The dashboard writes a short ‘this week’ text from your synced events — ready to paste into a newsletter, a bulletin or a social post. Choose which day your week starts, press Copy draft, read it over and paste it. On the current app version the draft is written with AI help from your event titles, times and places; it is a draft, so check the dates before you send it.
  • Ask the Wix AI assistant (Aria). In the dashboard chat you can ask things like ‘what's on this weekend?’, ‘is every calendar syncing?’ or ‘add a bake sale on Saturday at 10’. The assistant reads your calendar through the app and shows a preview before it creates anything.

Upgrade from the app dashboard; the plan is per site. Your calendars, settings and the widget stay exactly as they are.

For churches, schools and clubs

Can one calendar on the site show worship, classes and events separately?

Yes. Connect each Google Calendar or iCal feed as its own calendar (Pro: up to ten). Each gets its own color and a filter chip visitors can tap; on Pro you can also give events categories and filter by those.

How do we get this week's events into our bulletin or newsletter?

Schedule Assistant writes a short ‘this week’ text from your synced events. Pick the day your week starts, press Copy draft, read it over and paste it into your bulletin, newsletter or notice board.

We don't use Wix Events. Can we still show our schedule on the site?

Yes. Any Google Calendar, Outlook or iCal feed works, and you can add events yourself in the dashboard. Nothing else needs to be installed on the site.

Troubleshooting

"This needs the CMS app on your site"

The app stores your calendars in your site's CMS. Add CMS from the Wix App Market (free), then reload the app dashboard.

Events are missing or out of date

  • Check the Status column in the dashboard. An error shows the reason (for example a Google calendar that is not public, or an address that no longer answers). Fix the source and click Sync now.
  • Only events from about one month ago to twelve months ahead are shown.
  • A very large calendar shows Partial: descriptions are dropped first, then the calendar is capped at 1,500 events.
  • Visitors see a cached copy that refreshes hourly (Free) or every 5 minutes (Pro).

Times look wrong

Set the widget's time zone to the calendar's zone if your visitors and your events are in different places. Feeds that omit time-zone definitions are interpreted with the IANA database.

The Week or Day view does not appear

Those views are part of Pro; on the Free plan the widget shows the month grid instead.
